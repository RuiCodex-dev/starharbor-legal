Privacy Policy

Star Harbor · com.ruicodex.starharbor · RuiCodex · 2026-09-29

This policy explains how Star Harbor handles your data.

Data we collect

Star Harbor needs no sign-up and never asks for your name, email address, contacts or location. Your station, charters, Quanta, missions and settings are stored on your device.

To stay free the game shows advertising through Google AdMob, sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play, sends anonymous usage events to our own analytics server and, if you have an internet connection, lists your progress on an online leaderboard. Each is described below.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard runs on RuiCodex's own server (Appwrite, operated by RuiCodex, not shared with third parties). The game creates an anonymous account for your installation — no email, name or phone number — and stores only: a nickname (a generated "Captain-1234" or one you choose), the number of charters you completed, your station level, your singularity cores and the sector you reached. Other players can see your nickname and these numbers. Test devices used by Google Play's pre-launch checks never appear on the board.

You can hide any player from your own board and report an offensive nickname. A report stores only the reported entry's ID and nickname; RuiCodex reviews reports and resets or removes offending names. Nicknames that match our block list are never displayed.

You can remove your entry at any time in Settings › Remove me from the leaderboard; to delete the anonymous account as well, write to info@ruicodex.com.

Game analytics

To learn which charters are too hard or too easy and where players get stuck, the game sends usage events to our own analytics server (PostHog, operated by RuiCodex on its own infrastructure and not shared with third parties): for example that a charter or delivery was completed, which screens were opened and whether the store was visited. The events are linked to a random identifier created on your device — not to your name, email address or advertising ID — and are used only to improve the game. Session recording is not used. To have this data deleted, write to info@ruicodex.com.

Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To deliver them, AdMob processes your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, device and operating-system information, and your interaction with ads. We only receive aggregated reports. Buying "Remove Ads" or the Admiral Pass turns off banner and interstitial ads; optional reward videos remain available if you choose to watch them.

Google's policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game stores on your device which items you own so they keep working; Google Play restores durable items (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Admiral Pass) on a new installation.

In-app feedback

The feedback form records only the category you pick and your optional vote. If you write a message, your own email app opens and you decide whether to send it to info@ruicodex.com. Feedback is separate from store ratings; the game never asks you for a particular rating.

Consent and your choices

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent form before the first ad. You can refuse personalised ads; ads are then shown without personalisation. Where required, you can change your choice later under Profile › Ad privacy choices.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings › Google › Ads. "Erase all data" in the game's settings erases your progress on the device; uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. Ads are limited to the "G" (general audiences) content rating.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish KVKK and comparable laws you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing. Write to info@ruicodex.com; we answer within 30 days.
